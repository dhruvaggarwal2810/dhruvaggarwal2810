## `02` · About.exe

I'm **Dhruv Aggarwal**, a second-year **Electronics & Communication Engineering** student who can't stop making things.

Class teaches me how signals, circuits and systems work. Outside class, I build websites and run **Elivate**, an eyewear brand, where I get to practice the other half of building: design, product, and what a customer actually experiences. I'm still early in all of it, and I like it that way. Every build is a chance to learn something I couldn't get from a textbook.
